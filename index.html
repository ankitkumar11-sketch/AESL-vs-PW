Three findings worth acting on.
01 · VOLUME

Vijayawada is a PW volume stronghold

PW enrolls 702 students in Vijayawada against AESL's 329 — more than double. In Vizag the position reverses entirely: AESL holds 1,078 students to PW's 529. Whatever is winning share in Vizag isn't yet reaching Vijayawada.

02 · PRICING

AESL's price edge is real, but city-specific

In Vijayawada, AESL commands ₹64,288 average ARPU against PW's ₹58,220 — a ₹6,000+ premium AESL sustains despite lower volume. In Vizag, that pricing edge nearly disappears; PW's ARPU (₹57,902) edges slightly ahead of AESL's (₹56,789).

03 · WHITESPACE

Foundation in Vijayawada is open territory

AESL has zero Foundation students in Vijayawada while PW has enrolled 66 at a relatively low ₹27,112 ARPU. It's the one cohort-city cell AESL isn't competing in at all — and the cheapest entry point into the funnel PW is using to build volume.
